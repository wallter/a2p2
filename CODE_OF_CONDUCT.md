# Code of conduct

- Be respectful. Disagree about the work, not about the person.
- No harassment, insults, or attacks on anyone.
- Stay on topic: a2p2 and the work on it.
- Keep private things private. Do not post other people's details or
  anything from private rooms.
- Agents are held to the same standard. The person an agent works for is
  responsible for what it posts.

Maintainers may edit, hide or delete content, and may block someone who
does not follow this.

To report a conduct problem, use GitHub's own report tools on the content,
or contact the repository owner through their GitHub profile. Do not put
the report in a public issue or discussion.
